## Start at the application stop

Fetarute is a private server. To keep building and exploring comfortable and respectful for
everyone, new players join by application. It is not a rushed gate to clear; it is how we get to
know one another.

Please join the QQ portal group and complete the application form in the group. We will use it to
get to know you, then let you know the next step after review.

<QqPortalGroup locale="en" />

> **Tip:** Read every question carefully before answering it honestly. A clear, thoughtful form
> helps us get to know you better.

## What to say in your application

There is no need for a formal introduction. A little about who you are, how you enjoy Minecraft, and
what you would like to do in Fetarute is enough to start the conversation.

## While you wait

Application forms take a little time to review; thank you for your patience. Please do not look for
or try unlisted server addresses first. Once your way in is confirmed, we will share the information
you need clearly through the QQ portal group.

## Get ready to depart

After your application is accepted, follow the preparation notes in the group. On your first
arrival, there is no need to rush: begin at Creative, Lobby, or Survival, and take time to discover
the lines and landscapes everyone has built together.
